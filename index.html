07

How the draw is kept fair

  1. All 48 teams are ordered by the FIFA World Ranking (April 2026) — the final ranking before the tournament.
  2. Teams are split into seeded pots: Pot 1 holds one top team for every player, Pot 2 the next band, and so on. Everyone draws one team from every pot.
  3. Each pull is a random draw within its pot — completely random, but the favourites are always spread out. Nobody can land both of the top two seeds.
  4. If 48 doesn't split evenly, the leftover teams become extra pulls from the bottom band — who gets them is decided inside the same seeded draw, so it's reproducible from the draw ID (and the lucky dippers get a shot at the Wooden Spoon money 🥄).
