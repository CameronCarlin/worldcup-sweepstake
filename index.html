How the draw is kept fair

  1. All 48 teams are ordered by the FIFA World Ranking (April 2026) — the final ranking before the tournament.
  2. Teams are split into seeded pots. Pot 1 holds one top team for every player; Pot 2 the next band, and so on. Your 1st entry draws from Pot 1, your 2nd from Pot 2…
  3. Every entry is a random draw within its pot — completely random, but the favourites are always spread out. Nobody can land both of the top two seeds.
  4. Extra entries draw from the lower pots — fancying seconds buys the underdogs (and a shot at the Wooden Spoon money 🥄).
